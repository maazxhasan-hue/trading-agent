# Strategy Validation and Live Readiness

## Strategy flow

The NSE/MCX company evaluates its existing momentum, mean-reversion, event-driven, commodity-specialist, and cross-market-arbitrage votes against one timestamped market snapshot. It records attributable proposals and rejection reasons. Debate and portfolio risk remain shared context; a proposal does not independently authenticate or submit an order.

Proposal costs are explicit round-trip estimates. Configure `STRATEGY_COST_FEES_BPS`, `STRATEGY_COST_SPREAD_BPS`, `STRATEGY_COST_SLIPPAGE_BPS`, and `STRATEGY_COST_TAXES_BPS` from documented, conservative assumptions before relying on proposal net edge. Missing or invalid estimates reject proposals. These estimates do not prove realized execution costs.

## Offline cost-aware evaluation

`strategy_validation.net_walk_forward_report` evaluates realized per-strategy trade records chronologically. It needs complete `fees`, `spread`, `slippage`, and `taxes` values for every row, plus execution status, timestamp, gross realized P&L, turnover, and a regime label. Pass the candidate rows with `strategy_id` and comparable baseline rows identified as `baseline`:

```python
import json
from strategy_validation import net_walk_forward_report

with open("trade-evaluation.json", encoding="utf-8") as stream:
    data = json.load(stream)

report = net_walk_forward_report(
    data["history"],
    strategy_id=data["strategy_id"],
    baseline_trades=data["baseline_trades"],
)
print(json.dumps(report, indent=2, sort_keys=True))
```

The evaluator uses full chronological test windows after the training prefix. It reports net P&L after all four cost categories, expectancy, drawdown, turnover, per-window and per-regime results, and comparison to the baseline. It rejects missing costs or regime labels, malformed rows, insufficient sample/window counts, losses, unstable regimes, or a missing/weaker baseline. The final incomplete test window is excluded. Supply only realized records with comparable timestamps and accounting conventions; the function does not fetch broker data or establish that the supplied records are genuine.

`paper_only_qualified` means only that the supplied offline records passed these research checks. `live_eligible` is always `false`. The ₹1,000/hour figure is reported as `RESEARCH_ONLY`; changing it cannot alter qualification or authorize trading. It is not a forecast or promise. With ₹100, trading may be infeasible after actual lot size, margin, and costs; no trade is a valid outcome.

## Paper operation and live-readiness boundary

Keep `LIVE_TRADING=false` and `ANGELONE_READONLY=true`. Do not use a successful import, report, CI run, or paper result as authorization to arm a broker. Strategy proposals generated by the production company carry `validation_version="paper-only"`; the live signal path rejects them before funds lookup. The existing order manager also requires broker-derived quote, margin, exposure, daily-P&L, market-session, protective-exit, and reconciliation evidence, and fails closed when evidence is missing.

Before any separate controlled live test, an operator must:

1. Review Angel One account/API policy and current applicable exchange/SEBI requirements with official sources or support.
2. Confirm any required static-IP registration and VM public-IP configuration through the broker/operator channel.
3. Accumulate reproducible paper and out-of-sample evidence with actual fees, taxes, slippage, contract lot sizes, and regime labels.
4. Choose and review a daily loss limit and all exposure limits for the account; verify broker funds, session status, protective exits, and reconciliation.
5. Keep the VM and live mode untouched until a separate operator approval for one controlled authentication test. Never use a test order to validate authentication.

This repository change does not deploy to the VM, authenticate to Angel One, change a service, or submit an order. The repo's systemd example remains read-only with live trading disabled. A PR or green CI does not establish VM readiness or trading profitability.
